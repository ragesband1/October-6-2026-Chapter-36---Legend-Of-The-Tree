Once upon a time in a far far home, There lived a “Tree Of Life” where in case of this scenario. The wizard name Zayapath living there is respecting to take care the Mother Nature. As long as to keep a survival on Earth.

Suddenly came there is the wood cutters whom known the human and with the chainsaw. Wants to cut down the forests or likewise a trees. Wizard Zayapath didn’t know about the trees cut down. Because he is fell asleep in just 3 months ago of becoming hibernated person with lack of eating food.

The human wood cutters still cut down trees as never ending and what they do. Is just turning the cutting trees into papers. Like they want to draw, write and even putting the news all about. And because of so much abuse done by the human wood cutters with a chainsaw. After 3 months earlier in morning Wizard Zayath get awake and wanted to see the nature of trees around. Definitely it was already no more trees because it’s too late.

Wizard Zayapath said to himself: "How can it be, why this is happened. I’m sure this is what the people who’ve been abuse in cutting over and over trees all around without quitting their own part of job."

Wizard Zayapath wanted to figure it out who is the people cutting the trees. Then for his own intuition he wanted to punish the people on what is the reason why they cut down trees. After the next 2 months of finding. Wizard Zayapath peeped down the bushes below. Then he saw the human wood cutters who wanted to cut the last tree. Which is the home of Wizard Zayapath. He called it that time “Legend Of The Tree”.

Human saw the last tree and said: "Oh hmm… I think that is so nice to see. Even we need money to accumulate more, we must be greedy to cut down trees all along."

Wizard Zayapath already appeared on their front of the last “Legend Of The Tree”. Wizard Zayapath said: "Hey you! All of you why you are cutting down the trees! Don’t you know if you cut down the trees. You didn't care our healthy nature therefore when it is raining and due to the cause of bad weather with a storm and heavy rainfalls. All of the homes you live will been flooded. As the punishment I wanted to turn you into the 'Legend Of The Tree'. As the punishment both of you will turn into a single tree!"

The human wood cutters with a chainsaw was so scared and said: "Excuse me we we’re not afraid what you tell us... We want some gold and money. That’s why we cut down trees."

Wizard Zayapath turned them into the "Legend Of The Tree", where all human wood cutters transformed into a single magical tree. His home became 2 tree homes now...
